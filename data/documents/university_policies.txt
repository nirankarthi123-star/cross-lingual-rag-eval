# University Academic Regulations and Student Policies

## Section 1: Admission and Enrollment Eligibility
Undergraduate candidates must have achieved a minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 3.0 out of 4.0 or an equivalent 75% aggregate in their secondary school certification examinations. International applicants whose primary medium of education was not English are required to present valid language proficiency scores: either an IELTS Academic overall band of at least 6.5 with no individual band below 6.0, or a TOEFL iBT score of 85 or above. Candidates from bilingual curricula must submit official transcripts translated into English by a certified authority.

## Section 2: Grading System and Academic Standing
Academic performance is assessed using a 10-point scale or 4-point grade point system. A letter grade of 'A' corresponds to numerical scores between 90 and 100, carrying a 4.0 grade point value. A grade of 'B' corresponds to 80-89 (3.0), 'C' corresponds to 70-79 (2.0), 'D' represents a conditional pass for 60-69 (1.0), and 'F' represents failure below 60. Students must maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.50 to remain in satisfactory academic standing. Failure to do so across two consecutive academic trimesters triggers academic probation.

## Section 3: Scholarship Disbursement and Renewal Guidelines
Merit-based scholarships cover up to 50% of annual tuition fees and require recipient students to register for at least 15 credit hours per semester. To renew the scholarship annually, scholars must maintain a minimum CGPA of 3.60 and have zero disciplinary violations. Need-based financial assistance applications open annually on June 1st and require submission of authenticated family tax filings, bank declarations, and income verification documents.

## Section 4: Examination Conduct and Integrity Policy
All end-term examinations are invigilated under strict academic honesty codes. Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, possession of electronic smart devices inside the examination hall, or copying from unapproved study aids results in immediate annulment of the candidate's paper and referral to the University Disciplinary Tribunal. Decisions rendered by the tribunal may include academic suspension or permanent expulsion from the university.
